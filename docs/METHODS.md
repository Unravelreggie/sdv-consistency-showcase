# Evidence-to-decision methods

The private system's inspected capabilities include workbook ingestion, source images, OCR, structured extraction, deterministic checks, rule explanations and reviewer routing. Extraction and decision are separate stages. Identity candidates need source traceability and an eligible person role; a staff/guardian label is not automatically the subject's identity.

This showcase independently implements a small fictional subset: exact matching of a fabricated participant label that occurs in fabricated text; a toy enquiry/reply chain; positive finite system call duration; and non-failing informational treatment of an uncovered consent form. It has six invented examples and makes no OCR or LLM call. Its substring traceability check is deliberately simple and is not the production offset/evidence contract, multilingual name matching or full screenshot logic.

The result has two demo categories (consistent/inconsistent); manual review is a handling flag, not a third final category. A local acknowledgement does not change the rule decision or create a persistent audit record. No accuracy/performance or real patient outcome is presented. This toy logic is not clinical validation or operational acceptance.
